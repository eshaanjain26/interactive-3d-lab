    How each drone decides

    • Separation. Steer away from any drone inside the separation distance, weighted by 1/d².
    • Alignment. Match the average velocity of neighbors inside the perception radius.
    • Cohesion. Steer toward the local center of mass of those same neighbors.
    • Avoidance. Signed-distance checks against cubes and spheres, with a look-ahead probe and a slide along the surface.

    No drone sees the whole swarm. Each one reads only the neighbors a spatial hash returns for its own cell block. Order Φ is the length of the mean heading vector: 1.0 means every drone flies the same direction.
