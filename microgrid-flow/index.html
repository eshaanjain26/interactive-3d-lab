Dispatch order

  1. Renewables serve load first.
  2. Surplus charges the battery (up to 3 MW, tapering above 87% SOC).
  3. Deficit is covered by battery discharge down to the 10% reserve.
  4. The utility tie exports up to 2.5 MW or imports up to 3 MW.
  5. Anything left is curtailed (surplus) or shed (industrial first, homes last).
